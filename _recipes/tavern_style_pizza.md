## Philosophy
Exploring dough hydration and fermentation timing. Cold ferment develops complex flavor, but quick rise works when you're impatient. The hot honey drizzle transforms it from standard to 
craveable.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- Bread flour | 500 g |
- Water | 350 mL | 65–70% hydration
- Instant yeast | 6 g | ~2 tsp
- Sugar | 1 tsp |
- Salt | 1.5 tsp |
- Olive oil | 3 tbsp |

### Assembly
- Pizza sauce | ~1 cup |
- Mozzarella cheese | shredded, to taste |
- Toppings | your choice |
- Hot honey | for drizzling |
- Extra olive oil | for pan and brushing |

## Process

### Dough Preparation
1. Mix flour, water, yeast, sugar, salt, and olive oil together in bowl
2. If dough feels sticky: mix well → rest 20 min → then knead
3. Knead until smooth and stretchy (windowpane test: stretch a small piece thin enough to see light through without tearing)

### Fermentation Decision Point

**Option A: Cold Ferment (Recommended)**
- Refrigerate 24 to 48 hrs for best flavor development
- Bring to room temp 3 to 4 hours before baking

**Option B: Quick Rise**
- Rise 2 hours at room temp until doubled
- Skip cold fermentation entirely

### Tavern-Style (Thin & Crispy)

1. Divide dough into **two equal balls**
2. Oil a 9×11 rectangular pan generously
3. Stretch one dough ball to fit pan—if it resists, rest 5–10 min and try again
4. Cover and rest 30–45 min until slightly puffy
5. Brush more oil on top + edges for extra crisp
6. Preheat oven to 500°F
7. **Par-bake:** Bake 8 min (no toppings, just dough)
8. Remove and cool on rack 2-3 minutes
9. Add pizza sauce + cheese + toppings
10. Drizzle small amount of hot honey (optional at this stage)
11. Bake another 6–7 min until edges are crispy and cheese is bubbly
12. **Finish:** Drizzle hot honey generously over finished pizza

### Sicilian Variation (Thick & Fluffy)

1. Use **ONE full dough ball** (don't divide)
2. Before refrigerating: let rise 1 hour at room temp
3. Then refrigerate as usual (or skip to quick rise)
4. Follow same stretching, resting, and baking method as tavern-style
5. May need 1-2 extra minutes in final bake due to thickness

## Experiment Notes
- **Cold ferment results:** More complex, slightly tangy flavor. Crust develops better chew and crisp.
- **Hydration matters:** 65% = easier to handle. 70% = crispier but stickier to work with.
- **Hot honey timing:** Small drizzle before final bake distributes flavor. Heavy drizzle after baking gives concentrated pockets of sweet-spicy.
- **Pan choice:** Darker pans = crispier bottom. Shiny aluminum = softer crust.
- **Oil is key:** Don't skimp on the pan oil—it fries the bottom of the crust for that signature crunch.
