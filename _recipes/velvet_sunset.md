## Philosophy
A silky, tropical dessert exploring layered textures—mango brightness, custard velvet, and blueberry finish. This is about patience and temperature control for clean layers.

## Ingredients

### Mango Layer
- Mango purée | ½ to ¾ cup | fresh or canned
- Sugar | 1 tsp | adjust to taste
- Lemon juice | 1 tsp |
- Gelatin | 1 tsp (≈3 g) | bloomed

### Velvet Custard Layer
- Warm water | 2 tbsp | for blooming gelatin
- Milk | 2 cups (480 ml) |
- Custard powder | 2 tbsp | vanilla or mango flavor
- Sugar | 2 tbsp | or to taste
- Condensed milk | 1–2 tbsp | optional, for richness
- Egg | 1 large | lightly whisked
- Gelatin | 1 packet (8 g) | bloomed in 3 tbsp water
- Vanilla essence | 1 tsp | or pineapple essence

### Blueberry Glaze
- Blueberries | ¼ cup | fresh or frozen
- Sugar | 1 tsp |
- Lemon juice | squeeze |

## Process

### Mango Base
1. Bloom gelatin in warm water for 5 minutes
2. Warm mango purée with sugar and lemon juice gently
3. Stir in bloomed gelatin until completely dissolved
4. Pour into glasses or molds
5. Chill until lightly set (20–30 minutes)—should be tacky but not liquid

### Velvet Layer
1. In saucepan, whisk together milk, custard powder, sugar, and condensed milk
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until slightly thickened
3. **Tempering step:** Whisk egg in small bowl. Add 2-3 spoonfuls of hot custard to egg while whisking, then pour egg mixture back into pan
4. Cook on low heat for 1 minute more (do NOT boil or egg will scramble)
5. Remove from heat, add vanilla/pineapple essence
6. Stir in bloomed gelatin until fully dissolved
7. **Critical:** Strain through fine-mesh sieve for silky texture
8. Let cool to warm (not hot) before pouring over mango layer—too hot will melt the mango layer
9. Cover and refrigerate overnight (6–8 hours minimum)

### Blueberry Glaze
1. Heat blueberries, sugar, and lemon juice in small pan
2. Simmer until berries burst and sauce thickens (3-5 minutes)
3. Cool completely before serving

### Assembly
- Spoon blueberry glaze on top right before serving
- If using molds: unmold by inverting onto plate, then add glaze
- If using glassware: add glaze directly on top

## Experiment Notes
- Temperature control is everything—too-hot custard will melt the mango layer
- Straining the custard removes any egg bits for perfect smoothness
- Overnight chill allows gelatin to fully set and flavors to meld
- Could try passion fruit layer instead of mango for more tartness
